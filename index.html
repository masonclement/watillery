About Watillery

We started Watillery in 2025, in Ontario, because a good water fight deserves better gear. Every soaker we sell is picked and beaten on by the same small crew that grew up having these fights and never stopped.

We were tired of blasters that leaked down your arm, jammed on the second pump, or cracked in half by August. So we started sourcing our own and running each one through a full summer before deciding whether to sell it.

We fill the tanks and leave them overnight to check the seals. We run the batteries flat and charge them back up. We drop them on the driveway and hand them to kids. The ones that keep working are the ones you see on the site.
