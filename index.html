About Watillery

Watillery started in 2025 with a small Ontario crew that grew up having water fights and never really stopped. We were tired of blasters that leaked, jammed, or snapped in half halfway through summer, so we started sourcing and testing our own.

Everything we sell has been in our own hands first. We fill the tanks, run the batteries down, drop them on the driveway, and hand them to kids to see what breaks. The ones that hold up are the ones you see here.

We are still a tiny team, so the person answering your email is usually the same person who picked the product. That is the way we like it.
