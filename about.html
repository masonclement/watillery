About Watillery

Water guns and aquatic toys, built by people who still get in the fight.

Watillery got started in 2025 by a small crew in Ontario who grew up soaking each other in the backyard and never really grew out of it. Every summer we would buy the biggest, meanest looking blasters we could find, and every summer at least one of them leaked down our arm, jammed on the second pump, or cracked in half by August. We figured we could do better.

So we started bringing in blasters ourselves and putting them through a real summer before deciding whether to sell them. We fill the tanks and leave them overnight to check the seals. We run the batteries flat and charge them back up. We drop them on the driveway, hand them to kids, and take them to the lake. The ones that come back working are the ones that end up on the site.

The range keeps growing. Alongside the water guns we carry aquatic toys like remote-controlled submarines, manta rays and spouting whales, and most of the electric blasters now come with LED lighting, rechargeable packs and high-capacity tanks so a fight lasts longer than the walk back to the hose. If there is something you want us to track down, tell us. We are small enough that a good suggestion actually gets acted on.

We are still a tiny team, which means the person who answers your email is usually the same person who chose and tested the product. We would rather keep it that way.
